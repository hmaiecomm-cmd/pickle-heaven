DUPR Club Application & Website Integration Inquiry

【信件主旨】
DUPR Club, Rated Events and Website Integration Inquiry – [Venue Name], Taiwan

【收件人】
clubs@mydupr.com

【信件正文】

Dear DUPR Club Support Team,

We are writing on behalf of [Venue Name], a pickleball facility based in [City], Taiwan. We operate a dedicated facility with our own membership system, online booking platform, and event management system. We are very interested in becoming a registered DUPR Club and running DUPR-rated events at our venue.

We would like to understand the process and requirements for the following:

**1. Club Registration & Management**
- What is the process for a new venue to apply for or confirm DUPR Club status?
- If our club already has a Club ID, how do we designate a Club Director and add Club Organizers?
- What are the specific roles and permissions for each position (Club Director vs. Club Organizer)?

**2. Event Formats & Scoring**
- Which event formats can we submit for DUPR ratings? For example:
  - Doubles round-robin tournaments
  - Doubles bracket tournaments
  - Mixed league competitions
  - Are any of these formats restricted or require prior approval?
- What are the specific rules or restrictions for each format?
- Do we need to use any official DUPR tools, apps, or certified scorekeeping methods?

**3. Registration & Score Submission**
- Can we accept registrations on our own website and then manually enter or upload player data to DUPR?
- If so, what is the official CSV template or format for uploading scores?
- What are the required fields (player names, DUPR IDs, match results, timestamps, etc.)?
- What is the process for correcting scores after submission?
- How do we handle score disputes or rule violations?
- Is there a deadline for submitting scores after an event ends?

**4. Website Integration & API Access**
- What are the options for integrating member DUPR data into our website?
  a) Is there an official API we can apply for to allow members to link their DUPR accounts and display ratings?
  b) Are there approved integration partners or third-party solutions (e.g., Picklr, Sportime)?
  c) What is the application process, including testing environment, timeline, and approval criteria?
- What data can we request and display to members (DUPR ID, current rating, match history, etc.)?
- What are the privacy and consent requirements for displaying member DUPR data publicly?

**5. Branding & Marketing**
- How can we refer to events on our website? Can we use terms like "DUPR-Rated Event" or "DUPR Rating Event"?
- What DUPR logos and branding materials can we use in our marketing?
- Are there restrictions on using DUPR's name or logos in our event descriptions?
- Under what circumstances can we claim official approval or certification from DUPR?

**Additional Information**

We plan to host our first event in [Month/Date], and we expect [Event Format] with approximately [Number of Players] participants.

Please find our venue details below:

Anticipated Event Format:    [Format and player count]
Estimated Monthly Events:    [Frequency estimate, e.g., 2-4 events]

We would greatly appreciate any guidance on the process and timeline. We are committed to following all DUPR rules and ensuring a professional, fair experience for our members.

Thank you for your time, and we look forward to your response.

親愛的 DUPR Club 支援團隊，

我們代表位於台灣[城市]的[場館名稱]致函。本場館擁有自己的會員系統、線上訂場平台及活動報名系統。我們非常有興趣成為 DUPR 認可的俱樂部，並在場館舉辦 DUPR 評分賽。

我們想了解以下內容的流程與要求：

**1. 俱樂部申請與管理**
- 新場館如何申請或確認 DUPR 俱樂部資格？
- 若我們已有 Club ID，如何指定主辦人（Club Director）並新增協辦人（Club Organizer）？
- 各角色的權限分別是什麼（主辦人與協辦人的差異）？

**2. 比賽形式與計分規則**
- 我們可以提交哪些比賽形式進行 DUPR 評分？例如：
  - 雙打循環賽
  - 雙打淘汰賽
  - 聯賽制
  - 其中有限制或需事先核准的嗎？
- 每種形式的具體規則或限制是什麼？
- 是否需要使用 DUPR 官方工具、App 或認證的記分方式？

**3. 報名與成績提交**
- 我們可以在自家官網接受報名，然後手動輸入或上傳成績資料嗎？
- 若可以，官方提供哪種 CSV 格式或模板？
- 必填欄位包括哪些（球友名字、DUPR ID、比分、時間戳記等）？
- 提交後如何修正比分？
- 如何處理比分爭議或規則違反？
- 比賽結束後多久內需提交成績？

**4. 官網整合與 API 合作**
- 我們可以如何在官網整合會員的 DUPR 資料？
  a) 是否有官方 API 供申請，讓會員連結 DUPR 帳號並顯示評分？
  b) 是否有認可的整合合作方或第三方解決方案（例 Picklr、Sportime）？
  c) 申請流程、測試環境、時程及審核條件是什麼？
- 我們可取用及顯示哪些會員 DUPR 資料（ID、當前評分、比賽紀錄等）？
- 公開顯示會員 DUPR 資料時的隱私及同意要求是什麼？

**5. 品牌與行銷用語**
- 我們在官網如何稱呼比賽？可以寫「DUPR 評分賽」或「DUPR 認證賽」嗎？
- 可以使用哪些 DUPR 標誌和品牌素材進行行銷？
- 在活動說明中使用 DUPR 名稱或標誌有限制嗎？
- 什麼情況下可以宣稱獲得 DUPR 官方認可或認證？
